The material problem

What's actually
in your tea bag?

Many everyday tea bags use a sealing material that includes plastic — something most people never see and never chose. It's a quiet, common part of how tea bags are usually made.

We designed it out.

Patti's tea bags are built to be plastic-free and biodegradable from the start, without changing how you brew or how the tea tastes.

Patti Intelligence.

Technology that supports the craft

Three ideas, working as one system.

01

Patti AI

A customer-facing conversational assistant that helps you find your tea, brew it correctly, and understand why Patti is built the way it is.

02

Patti Tea Intelligence

A prototype quality-screening system that uses computer vision to examine the visible characteristics and consistency of tea samples — one input among many in how tea is checked, not a replacement for it.

03

Patti Learning Loop

Tea → customer → data → AI → insight → better decisions → better tea. A loop that feeds what we learn back into what we grow, source and pack.

India already drinks chai every day. We wanted to make that better.

Patti started with a simple observation: tea is already a daily ritual across India, brewed strong and milky, shared without much thought. The materials and the thinking behind it hadn't kept up.

So we set out to bring better leaves, better materials, and a little more intention to something people already do every single day — without asking anyone to change their chai.

Technology and sustainability aren't the headline here. The cup is. Everything else exists to make that cup a little better, and to leave a little more behind than it takes.
